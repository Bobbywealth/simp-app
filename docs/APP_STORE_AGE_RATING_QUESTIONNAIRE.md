# App Store Age Rating Questionnaire — SIMP

App Store Connect asks a series of Yes/No questions to assign an age rating. SIMP is a dating app, so the rating will be **17+** (the highest non-gambling/non-unrestricted rating). Below are the expected answers to every question.

## How to fill this out

In App Store Connect → SIMP → Age Rating → edit the questionnaire. The questionnaire varies slightly by region but these answers apply globally.

## Answers

### Violence

- Cartoon or Fantasy Violence: **No**
- Realistic Violence: **No**
- Prolonged Graphic or Sadistic Violence: **No**
- Graphic Violence (realistic, blood, gore): **No**
- Violence toward defenseless / non-combatant characters: **No**

### Sexual Content / Themes

- Sexual Content or Nudity: **No** — SIMP does not contain explicit sexual content. Profile photos may include suggestive but not explicit imagery; users upload their own photos and are moderated.
- Sexual Content or Themes: **No**
- Graphic Sexual Content: **No**
- Sexual Violence: **No**
- Nudity (for non-sexual contexts like health/wellness): **No**
- Simulated Gambling: **No**
- Real Gambling: **No**

### Profanity / Crude Humor

- Profanity or Crude Humor: **Mild** (occasional). Users can type freely in their bio / messages; we don't allow slurs but some profanity is expected. Select "Mild" or "None" depending on the actual content at submission time. Review the most-recently active profiles for an honest answer.

### Horror / Fear

- Horror or Fear Themes: **No**

### Mature / Suggestive Themes

- Mature / Suggestive Themes: **Infrequent/Mild** — SIMP is a dating app, which is inherently suggestive. The reference to adult relationships and physical attraction is the core product. Apple generally classifies this as "Infrequent/Mild" because the explicit content is user-supplied, not platform-provided.

### Alcohol / Tobacco / Drugs

- Alcohol References: **No**
- Tobacco References: **No**
- Drugs References: **No**
- Non-User-Generated Medical/Treatment Information: **No**

### Gambling

- Real Gambling: **No**
- Simulated Gambling: **No**

### User-Generated Content

- Unrestricted Web Access: **No**
- User-Generated Content: **Yes**
- Messaging and Chat: **Yes**
- Age-Restricted User-Generated Content (users must be 18+): **Yes**

For "User-Generated Content," the form will then ask:
- Do you have a content-moderation policy? **Yes**
- Do you have a way for users to report objectionable content? **Yes**
- Do you have a way for users to block other users? **Yes**

### Authentication / Accounts

- Account Creation: **Yes**
- User Authentication: **Yes** (Sign in with Apple + email/password)

### Location

- Location Services: **Yes**
  - Coarse location only (city-level from IP)
  - Used for app functionality (showing distance to other members)
- No precise location (GPS) collection

### Privacy

- Collects personal data for tracking: **No**
- Collects personal data not for tracking: **Yes**

## Final rating

After submitting these answers, App Store Connect should auto-assign:
- **United States / most regions**: 17+
- **European Union / Germany / Australia**: 18+

Both ratings are required because SIMP is a dating app and explicitly excludes users under 18.

## Additional required attestations

When prompted, attest that:
- The app does not contain content that encourages minors to engage in inappropriate behavior
- The app is not targeted at minors
- The app does not contain user-generated content that could be accessed by minors
- The app does not contain frequent profanity
- The app does not contain horror content

## Why 17+

Apple's guideline for dating apps is to rate 17+:
- Dating apps are inherently adult content
- Apple requires 17+ for apps that involve meeting new people in person
- App Store Connect will pre-fill 17+ when "Dating" is selected as primary or secondary genre

## If the rating comes back lower

If App Store Connect returns less than 17+:
- Verify the primary genre is set to "Social" or "Lifestyle" — not "Entertainment"
- Verify the secondary genre is "Dating"
- Re-attest to the user-generated content questions above
- Contact Apple Reviewer via the resolution center to request re-review with explicit dating-app context

## Reference

Apple App Store Rating Council — current thresholds:
- 4+ : no objectionable content
- 9+ : mild cartoon/fantasy violence or crude humor
- 12+ : mild realistic violence, mild profanity
- 17+ : intense violence, sexual content, gambling, dating
- Unrated: gambling, frequent adult content

SIMP belongs at 17+ because it is a dating app that involves real-world meetings between adults.
