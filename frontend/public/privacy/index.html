Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-21 · Last updated: 2026-08-21

TL;DR. SIMP collects the data we need to match you with people, keep you safe, and run the service. You can export or delete your account at any time. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We share data only with the service providers we list below, only with law enforcement when legally required, and only with other users when you choose to make it public (e.g. your profile).

1. What SIMP is

SIMP ("Successful · Intentional · Male · Providers") is a curated dating, live, and messaging platform. We provide profile discovery, real-time video, direct messaging, and optional premium subscriptions (SIMP+ and SIMP Elite). The service is operated by Wolfpaq Marketing LLC and is intended for adults 18 and older.

2. Data we collect

We collect three categories of data: data you give us, data we collect automatically, and data from connected services.

2.1 Data you give us

  • Account: email address, display name, password (hashed with bcrypt), date of birth, and a self-attested age confirmation timestamp.
  • Profile: gender, the gender(s) you're interested in, optional city, optional occupation, optional height, and a written bio.
  • Photos: profile photos you upload (stored on Cloudinary; we never publish them off-platform).
  • Verification: if you apply for the blue verified badge, the selfie + ID images you submit. These are reviewed by a human moderator and then permanently deleted — they are never used to train models and never shared.
  • Content: messages you send, profile prompts you answer, and any reports you file.
  • Subscriptions: product IDs and receipt-validation metadata returned by Apple's App Store when you buy SIMP+ or SIMP Elite.

2.2 Data we collect automatically

  • Device: device model, OS version, push-notification token (APNs / FCM), preferred language.
  • Usage: which screens you visit, which profiles you view, swipes, likes, super-likes, boosts, and match outcomes (used to operate daily-rate limits and to compute recommendations).
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and error logs (Pino). We do not use third-party analytics SDKs that track you across other apps or websites.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP for showing nearby matches. We do not request precise GPS location by default.

2.3 Data from connected services

  • Sign in with Apple: we receive a stable Apple user ID and — only if you choose to share it — your name and a private-relay email address. We never see your real Apple ID email unless you opt in.
  • App Store receipts: Apple's App Store Server API tells us which subscription tier you have and whether it's active.

3. How we use your data

We use the data above to:

  • Create and authenticate your account.
  • Show your profile to other SIMP members (and theirs to you) for matching.
  • Send you messages, push notifications, and the rare but important safety or policy notice.
  • Detect abuse, enforce our Terms of Service, and respond to legal process.
  • Operate billing for SIMP+ / SIMP Elite via Apple's App Store. SIMP never sees your payment card number; that data lives entirely with Apple.
  • Improve the product via aggregated, de-identified analytics. We never sell raw or identifiable data.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling across other companies' apps, or to sell to data brokers.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the following categories of recipient, and only what is necessary for the service to work:

  • Other SIMP members: anything you put on your profile, plus your messages to the people you match with.
  • Service providers:
    • Cloudinary — photo storage.
    • Apple — push notifications (APNs), Sign in with Apple, App Store receipts.
    • Twilio — TURN relay for live-streaming.
    • Resend — transactional email (verification, password reset).
    • Render — application hosting and the production Postgres database.
    Each provider is contractually restricted to using the data only to provide services to us.
  • Law enforcement: only when we receive a valid subpoena, court order, or other legally binding demand. We publish an annual transparency report.
  • Acquirers: if Wolfpaq Marketing LLC is acquired or merges, your data would transfer under the same privacy commitments.

We do not sell, rent, or barter your personal data.

5. Your rights and controls

  • Edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • Export your data from Settings → Privacy → Export my data. You'll receive a JSON archive of your profile, photos, matches, and messages.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Privacy → Delete account. We hard-delete your profile, photos, matches, and messages within 30 days. Backups are rotated within 90 days. Reports and moderation actions are retained in anonymized form to prevent ban-evasion, but are no longer linked to your identity.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. optional location) at any time.
  • California residents have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sharing" as defined by the CCPA. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.
  • European / UK residents have the rights granted by the GDPR / UK GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Our lawful bases are: contract (to provide the service you signed up for), legitimate interests (to keep the platform safe and functional), and consent (for anything optional).

6. Children

SIMP is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that a user is under 18, we delete their account and any associated data. If you believe a child is using SIMP, please contact us at privacy@sim-p.app and we will act within 24 hours.

7. Security

We protect your data with: TLS for all traffic, bcrypt-hashed passwords, rotating JWT access tokens, hashed refresh tokens, rate-limited authentication endpoints, IP-hashing for audit trails, and a dedicated Trust & Safety team. Photos uploaded for verification are deleted immediately after review.

No system is 100% secure. If we discover a breach that materially affects you, we notify you and the relevant authorities within the timeframes required by law.

8. International transfers

SIMP is operated from the United States. By using the service you understand that your data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EEA / UK.

9. Cookies and tracking

The web version of SIMP uses a single first-party session cookie for authentication. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics cookies, or social-media tracking pixels.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app and ask you to accept the new policy before you can keep using SIMP. The "Effective" date above shows the most recent version.
