SIMP Support

Last updated: 2026-08-21

Need help right now?
The fastest way to get help is from inside the app: Settings → Help & Support → Contact us. This routes your message to the right team automatically — billing questions go to the Billing team, safety reports go to Trust & Safety, and so on. Replies typically arrive within 24 hours, faster for billing and safety.

1. Account & sign-in

  • I can't sign in. Try the password-reset flow at /forgot-password. If you signed up with Apple ID and don't remember which Apple ID you used, go to Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Sign in & Security → Apps Using Apple ID.
  • I want to change my email. Settings → Account → Email. For Apple-only accounts, we use a private-relay email, so you'll need to add a password first or contact support.
  • My account is locked. Too many failed sign-in attempts. It unlocks automatically after 30 minutes, or you can reset your password to unlock immediately.

2. Pricing & billing

SIMP is fully free. There is no subscription, no SIMP+ tier, no SIMP Elite, and no in-app purchase to subscribe to, cancel, or restore. The app does not store payment information of any kind.

  • Can I subscribe? No — every feature is included for free.
  • Is there a SIMP+ tier? No. The free tier includes everything: rewind, advanced filters, photo verification, live streaming.
  • I want to support SIMP. Thank you — but SIMP doesn't accept payments today. The best way to support the project is to tell a friend, leave an App Store review, or report a bug.
  • I see a charge I don't recognize. You shouldn't — please email support@sim-p.app with any screenshots and we'll identify it within one business day.

3. Safety & Trust

  • Report a user: open their profile → ⋯ → Report. Choose a reason. Reports are reviewed by humans within 24 hours.
  • Block a user: open their profile → � → Block. They will not appear in your discovery and cannot message you.
  • I'm in immediate danger: call 911 (US), 999 (UK), or your local emergency number first. Then use the in-app Report to flag the user. SIMP Trust & Safety is on call 24/7 for imminent-harm reports.
  • Someone is pressuring me for money, gifts, or intimate images. This is always a scam. Stop responding, block, and report. Read our Safety Center for the full playbook.
  • Under-18 user: report them via the in-app Report with reason "Underage." We remove the account and all associated data within 24 hours.

4. Technical issues

  • The app crashes on launch: force-quit → reopen. If it persists, reinstall (your data is on the server, not the device). If it still persists, contact support@sim-p.app with your device model + iOS version.
  • I don't get push notifications: iPhone → Settings → Notifications → SIMP → enable Allow notifications. In-app → Settings → Notifications → make sure each category is on.
  • Live stream is black for the viewer: usually a network firewall blocking WebRTC. We use Twilio's TURN relay for cross-network connectivity — if the issue persists, report the stream.
  • Photos won't upload: usually a storage or network issue. We store photos on Cloudinary; if upload fails, retry after a moment or switch networks.

5. Privacy & data

  • Export my data: Settings → Privacy → Export my data. You'll receive a JSON archive within 7 days.
  • Delete my account: Settings → Privacy → Delete account. Confirmation is required. Deletion completes within 30 days.
  • Privacy questions: privacy@sim-p.app. See the full Privacy Policy.

6. Reporting content / DMCA

If you believe content on SIMP infringes your copyright, send a DMCA notice to dmca@sim-p.app with: (a) identification of the copyrighted work, (b) the URL or screenshot of the allegedly infringing material, (c) your contact info, (d) a good-faith statement, (e) a statement under penalty of perjury that the notice is accurate and you are authorized to act for the copyright owner, and (f) your physical or electronic signature.
